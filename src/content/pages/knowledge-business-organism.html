AEVORA Knowledge / Definition

What is a business organism?

AEVORA uses business organism to describe a persistent digital system designed to understand an organization's approved operational context, prepare and coordinate work, retain continuity, and earn carefully bounded autonomy through demonstrated reliability.

Core distinction

Persistent context rather than an isolated response.

The intended system follows open work, decisions, constraints, and outcomes across approved business tools. It starts by observing and preparing decision-ready work, rather than assuming authority to operate the company.

Scope

Useful before autonomous.

Organism is AEVORA's design term. It does not mean biological life or establish that today's prototype has achieved autonomous cognition. Early work remains human-governed and evidence-gated.
