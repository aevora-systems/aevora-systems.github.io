Privacy

Privacy by default while the website is still being established.

Aevora Website v0.5 does not include a live enquiry backend, advertising tracker or customer account system. This page should be revised before public launch whenever analytics, forms, cookies or third-party services are added.

Current website data collection

This static build does not intentionally collect form submissions or store visitor profiles. The pilot form is deliberately disabled until an official endpoint is configured.

Hosting logs

The eventual hosting provider may process ordinary server and security logs. The production privacy notice should name the provider and retention practices once hosting is selected.

Future analytics

If analytics are introduced, Aevora should prefer privacy-respecting measurement and disclose what is collected, why it is collected and how long it is retained.

Contact data

When a contact channel is connected, Aevora should publish a clear purpose for collecting enquiries and define access, retention and deletion procedures.

Pre-launch note: this is an implementation placeholder, not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal review once Aevora incorporates and launches publicly.
