# AEVORA SEO/GEO strategy — v0.5

**First principle:** Ensure real, public, crawlable source content is accurate. Generative search citations are not guaranteed by markup or special crawler instructions.

## Primary entity

AEVORA is a technology and research initiative founded by Jeremiah Wong Zhi Qi on 23 September 2026 and developing governed digital business organisms. The public website describes the operating direction and governance limits, not a proven autonomous product.

## Initial topic cluster

- Business organisms: scope, continuity and authorized operational context.
- Governed autonomy: capability is separate from authority.
- Research: actual recorded evidence and limitations, only after publication.
- Constitution: original first-party governing source.

## Technical foundation

Every indexed page has title, description, canonical, OG and URL-linked JSON-LD. Sitemap lists actual prerendered pages only. The GitHub Pages origin is used until a real custom domain exists. Schema definitions are intentionally conservative and correspond to visible content.

## Before production indexing

- Create the organization-root Pages repo and deploy successfully.
- Verify mobile and desktop motion behavior on the actual Pages URL.
- Validate sitemap/robots response codes and Search Console/Bing Webmaster ownership.
- Confirm no accidental draft/research disclosure.
- Wire a real enquiry destination and update privacy only when contact infrastructure exists.
- Measure crawl/index status and citations instead of promising search placement.

## Future publication template

Article heading, concise first-party definition, author, real publication/updated dates, context, method, results, limitations, evidence links and change history. No invented metrics, release dates or citations.
